How to make a kids' leggings

Slim leggings from a single pattern piece used for front and back. Under a skirt or on their own. Sew them in jersey with a stretch stitch. Cut them long and turn the hems up, and they still fit after a growth spurt.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one pair, 120 cm height)

  • Jersey (single knit, interlock or rib) — about 90 cm wide × 70 cm
  • Flat elastic, 2.5 cm — about 50 cm
  • A ballpoint needle (75/11) and stretch thread

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Lay the pattern on the wrong side and trace both lines. Run the stretchiest direction across the body.
  2. Cut the pieces
    Cut 2 fronts and 2 backs — 4 in all, from the same pattern piece.
  3. Transfer the notches
    Mark where the crotch curve ends.

4. Sewing

4-1. Sew the crotch curves

  1. Place the two fronts right sides together and sew the crotch curve. Same for the backs.
  2. Use a ballpoint needle and a stretch stitch — zigzag or a knit stitch.
  3. A plain straight stitch will snap the first time they are pulled on.

4-2. Sew the sides and inseam

  1. Place front and back right sides together and sew both sides.
  2. Then sew the inseam in one run, hem to hem.
  3. Backstitch over the crotch junction.

4-3. Thread the waist elastic

  1. Fold the waist into a casing and stitch, leaving a 3 cm gap.
  2. Thread the elastic, overlap the ends by 2 cm and stitch.
  3. Close the gap.

4-4. Hem the legs

  1. Fold each hem by 1 cm, then 2 cm, and stitch.
  2. Use a stretch stitch here too, or the foot will not go through.

✏️ Jersey needs a ballpoint needle, stretch thread and a stretch stitch. A plain straight stitch snaps the first time the leggings go on. If your machine only offers zigzag, a narrow one (1 mm wide, 2.5 mm long) works.

5. Tips & variations

  • Turn the hems up: cut long and turn them up, then let them down as the child grows.
  • Soft elastic: 2.5 cm or wider at 80% of the hip. Tighter presses on the tummy.
  • Make the set: pair them with the kids' tunic.
