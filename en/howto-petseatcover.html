How to make a pet car seat cover

A cover that goes over the back seat of a car, protecting it from hair and mud. Ties at the four corners fasten to the headrests and under the seat. In a water-repellent fabric it copes with wet paws.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one cover, standard car)

  • Fabric (water-repellent nylon or canvas) — about 150 cm wide × 130 cm (59 × 51 in)
  • Fabric for the ties — about 50 × 20 cm
  • Sewing thread

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

2. Make the pattern (auto-generated in Katagami)

With the Katagami pattern tool, you just enter the finished size and it drafts a seam-allowance-included pattern, tiled to print at actual size on Letter or A4.

  1. Open the tool
    Open the pattern tool and choose “Pets” → “Pet car seat cover” from the tabs at the top.
  2. Enter the size
    Enter the seat width, the seat depth, the backrest height and the front drop. Presets cover a kei car, a standard car and an MPV.
  3. Set the seam allowance
    Use the slider at the bottom (beginners: 1.0–1.5 cm / about 3/8–5/8 in).
  4. Print
    Press “Print (actual size)”, then in the print dialog set Scale = 100% and turn “Fit to page” OFF. Check the 50 mm calibration box on the first guide sheet with a ruler.

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Measure the car
    Seat width, seat depth and backrest height. These vary a great deal between models.
  2. Trace the pattern
    Lay the pattern on the wrong side and trace the cutting line. It is large — the floor is easier.
  3. Cut the pieces
    One body and four ties.
  4. Transfer the notches
    Mark the four tie positions and the fold lines.

4. Sewing

4-1. Hem the edges

  1. Turn all four sides under twice, 1 cm each time, and stitch.
  2. It is large, so press as you go and work in sections.

4-2. Make the ties

  1. Fold all four ties in four and topstitch.
  2. Water-repellent fabric slips — clips help.

4-3. Attach the ties

  1. Stitch a tie at each of the four corner notches.
  2. Reinforce with a stitched square and its diagonals. These are pulled hard.

4-4. Fit it

  1. Tie the top two to the headrests.
  2. Pass the lower two under the seat and tie.
  3. Pull out any slack.

✏️ Measure your own car. Even within a class, seat widths differ by more than 10 cm between models. The presets are a guide; a cover made to measured figures actually sits flat instead of shifting.

5. Tips & variations

  • Water-repellent fabric: after a wet walk, the seat stays dry.
  • Make it washable: untie it and it goes in the machine. Hair makes that essential.
  • Match the pet mat: made in the same fabric, the car looks of a piece.
