How to make baby pull-on pants

Pants with a rise deep enough to go over a diaper. Front and back use the same pattern piece, so you cut four and sew in three stages: crotch, sides and inseam, then elastic. Elastic at the hems as well as the waist means they stay put through crawling.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (size 80 cm)

  • Main fabric (jersey, double gauze or light cotton) — about 110 cm wide × 60 cm (44 × 24 in)
  • Flat elastic (1.5 cm / ⅝ in wide) — about 50 cm (20 in) for the waist
  • Narrow flat elastic (0.8 cm / ⅜ in wide) — about 40 cm (16 in) for the hems
  • Bodkin

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Trace the cut line, seam line and the crotch curve.
  2. Cut
    Cut two fronts and two backs from the same pattern, flipping it for the second of each pair.
  3. Finish the edges
    Zigzag or serge the allowances. Knit does not need it.

4. Sewing

4-1. Sew the crotch seams

  1. Sew the two fronts together along the center front curve, then the two backs.
  2. Clip the curves every 5 mm (¼ in) so they open flat.

4-2. Sides and inseams

  1. Sew both side seams, then the inseam in one run from hem to hem.
  2. Backstitch two or three times over the crotch junction.

4-3. Waist elastic

  1. Fold the waist edge over twice to make a casing and stitch, leaving a 3 cm (1¼ in) gap.
  2. Thread the 1.5 cm elastic to about 85% of the baby's waist, stitch the ends and close the gap.

4-4. Hems

  1. Make a casing at each hem the same way and thread the narrow elastic.
  2. Set it loosely around the calf for a soft, gathered leg — or skip the hem elastic entirely for straight legs.

✏️ The room for the diaper comes from the rise and the crotch extension. With cloth diapers, add 2–3 cm (1 in) to the rise beyond the preset. With disposables the preset is enough.

5. Tips & variations

  • Loose leg elastic: a baby's legs move constantly. The hem elastic should leave no mark at all.
  • Mark the back: front and back are the same shape, so a tag or a line of stitching inside the back waist saves confusion at 3 am.
  • Make a set: the same fabric as a bib or a baby hat.
