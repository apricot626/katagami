How to make a taggie square

A small square with ribbon tags caught all round the edge. Babies love fiddling with tags, and this one thing keeps them busy. Two squares sewn right sides together and turned. In toweling and gauze, the two faces feel completely different.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one 20 cm square)

  • Outer (double gauze) — about 25 × 25 cm
  • Lining (toweling or terry) — about 25 × 25 cm
  • Ribbon, 1–2.5 cm wide — 6 cm × 8 pieces (mixing types is more fun)
  • Hand-sewing thread

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Lay the pattern on the wrong side and trace both lines.
  2. Cut the pieces
    One outer, one lining.
  3. Transfer the notches
    Mark the tag positions on each side — two per side, eight in all, at the standard setting.
  4. Cut the ribbon
    Cut 6 cm lengths and seal the ends with a flame or fray stopper.

4. Sewing

4-1. Pin the tags

  1. Fold each ribbon in half to make a loop.
  2. Lay the loops on the right side of the outer, pointing inwards, with the cut ends level with the fabric edge.
  3. Pin or baste at the notches. Eight in all.
  4. Get the direction wrong and the tags end up inside after turning. Check that every loop points towards the middle.

4-2. Sew the two layers

  1. Lay the lining on top, right sides together, and sew around, leaving about 7 cm open along the bottom.
  2. Backstitch two or three times over each tag so it cannot pull out.
  3. Clip the rounded corners.

4-3. Turn and topstitch

  1. Turn right side out and check that every tag is on the outside.
  2. Fold the opening's allowance inwards and topstitch all the way round.
  3. Running a second line over the base of the tags makes them stronger still.

✏️ Babies put their whole weight into pulling on the tags. Backstitch over each one when you sew the seam, then catch them again with the topstitching. Seal the ribbon ends too — once they fray, they split from there.

5. Tips & variations

  • Mix the ribbons: satin, grosgrain, lace. Different textures keep it interesting.
  • Different face each side: gauze and toweling is the classic pairing — it changes as it is squeezed.
  • Clip it to the pram: make one tag longer (12 cm) and you can thread a clip through it so it never hits the floor.
