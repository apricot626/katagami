How to make a bunny ears headband

Tall, upright bunny ears on a headband. Sew two layers per ear, turn, top with a pink inner piece, and attach to a store-bought headband. Interfacing or wire inside lets the ears stand or bend to any angle. For Easter, recitals or Halloween.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (For the adult size (26 cm ear height))

  • Main fabric (white felt or boa) — about 40 × 60 cm (for 4 ear pieces)
  • Contrast fabric (pink, for the inner) — about 20 × 40 cm
  • Fusible interfacing or wire (to shape the ears) — a little
  • A store-bought headband — 1
  • Headband cover fabric (felt) — about 40 × 8 cm
  • Sewing / hand-sewing thread

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Prepare the headband
    Measure the length of your headband (end to end along the arc) and enter it; an ordinary one is about 30–40 cm.
  2. Cut
    Cut 4 outer ears (2 layers per ear), 2 pink inner pieces and one headband cover. Felt can be left with raw edges.

4. Sewing

4-1. Add the pink inner

  1. On the two ear pieces that will face front, sew a pink inner piece to the center, stitching all the way around like an applique.

4-2. Sew and turn the ears

  1. Place two ear layers right sides together and sew all the way around, leaving the base open. Clip the curves so they turn cleanly.
  2. Turn right side out through the base and insert interfacing or wire to hold the shape. Make the second ear the same way.

4-3. Attach to the headband

  1. Fold the cover fabric, wrap it over the store-bought headband and sew along the edge.
  2. Sew the two ears about 5 cm to each side of the center, tilting them slightly.

✏️ In felt the edges do not fray, so you can skip turning: stack two layers, sew around, and slip the interfacing between. Wire lets you fold one ear over for character.

5. Tips & variations

  • Change the color: white and pink is classic; brown for a bear, gray for a mouse, black for a black rabbit.
  • Pair with a tail: make the costume tail in white boa for a full bunny outfit.
  • For Easter: pair with a tutu skirt for an Easter bunny costume.
