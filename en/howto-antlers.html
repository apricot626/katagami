How to make a reindeer antlers headband

Branched reindeer antlers on a headband. Sew two layers per antler, turn, hold the shape with interfacing or wire, and attach to a store-bought headband. Brown felt or boa, for kids and adults — perfect for Christmas or a recital.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (For the adult size (20 cm antler height))

  • Main fabric (brown felt or boa) — about 40 × 50 cm (for 4 antler pieces)
  • Fusible interfacing or wire (to shape the antlers) — a little
  • A store-bought headband — 1
  • Headband cover fabric (felt) — about 40 × 8 cm
  • Sewing / hand-sewing thread

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Prepare the headband
    Measure the length of your headband (end to end along the arc) and enter it; an ordinary one is about 30–40 cm.
  2. Cut
    Cut 4 antler pieces (2 layers per antler, 2 antlers; mirror the pattern for the back pieces) and one headband cover. Felt can be left with raw edges.

4. Sewing

4-1. Sew and turn the antlers

  1. Place two antler layers right sides together and sew all the way around, leaving the base open. Clip the seam allowance at the inner branch valleys so it turns cleanly.
  2. Turn right side out through the base and insert interfacing or wire to hold the shape. Make a second antler the same way.

4-2. Cover the headband

  1. Fold the cover fabric, wrap it over the store-bought headband and sew along the edge. Fabric glue also works.

4-3. Attach the antlers

  1. Sew the two antlers about 5 cm to each side of the center of the headband, tilting them slightly outward.
  2. Whipstitch firmly around the base so they stay steady.

✏️ In felt the edges do not fray, so you can skip turning: just stack two layers, sew around, and slip the interfacing between them. Wire lets you bend the antlers into any pose.

5. Tips & variations

  • Change the color: brown for realistic antlers, white for a baby reindeer; a little beige at the tips looks more real.
  • Add ears: make the cat-ears pattern in brown and attach them too for a fuller reindeer look.
  • For pets: make it smaller for dog or cat reindeer antlers.
