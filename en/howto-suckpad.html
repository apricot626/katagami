How to make a carrier suck pads

Pads that wrap round the shoulder straps of a carrier to protect them from dribble. They fasten with snaps, so they come off the moment you want to wash them. Gauze on the outside and toweling inside gives you something that actually absorbs. Two pads make a pair.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one pair, standard size)

  • Outer (double gauze or Oxford) — about 25 × 50 cm
  • Lining (toweling or terry) — about 25 × 50 cm
  • Snap fasteners — 4 pairs
  • Sewing thread

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Measure the strap
    Measure the width of the carrier's shoulder strap and the length you want covered. The strap's circumference plus 2 cm gives the wrap width.
  2. Trace the pattern
    Lay the pattern on the wrong side and trace both lines.
  3. Cut the pieces
    Two outer and two lining — a pair.
  4. Transfer the notches
    Mark the snap positions on each side.

4. Sewing

4-1. Sew the two layers

  1. Place outer and lining right sides together and sew around, leaving about 7 cm open along the bottom.
  2. Clip the rounded corners.
  3. Make two.

4-2. Turn and topstitch

  1. Turn right side out and ease the corners.
  2. Fold the opening's allowance inwards and topstitch all the way round.

4-3. Add the snaps

  1. Wrap the pad round the actual strap and find the position where it sits snugly.
  2. Fit two pairs of snaps at that position.
  3. Too tight crushes the strap padding; too loose and it slides down. Check by wrapping before you set them.

✏️ Decide the snap positions on the real strap, not from the pattern. Carriers differ a great deal in how thick their straps are, and the drawn positions may not suit yours. Pin the pad closed first, confirm it does not slide, then set the snaps.

5. Tips & variations

  • Toweling inside: absorbing is the job. Terry or toweling on the inner face.
  • Make two pairs: a spare set means no waiting for the wash to dry.
  • Match the head rest: a cover for the head support in the same fabric completes the set.
