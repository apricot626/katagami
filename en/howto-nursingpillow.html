How to make a nursing pillow cover

A cover for a crescent nursing pillow. The opening is an envelope so it comes off easily for washing. Shop-bought pillows vary a lot between makers, so measure your own outer diameter, inner diameter and depth and enter those.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one cover, standard size)

  • Main fabric (double gauze, terry or Oxford) — about 110 cm wide × 80 cm (43 × 31 in)
  • Sewing thread
  • Optional: 2 pairs of snaps for the opening

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Measure the pillow
    Outer diameter, inner diameter and depth. Get these wrong and it will not go on.
  2. Trace the pattern
    Lay the pattern on the wrong side and trace both lines.
  3. Cut the pieces
    One top, one bottom. Cut the two gussets — outer and inner — slightly long.
  4. Transfer the notches
    Mark the ends of the opening.

4. Sewing

4-1. Attach the gussets

  1. Sew the outer gusset to the outer edge of the top piece, right sides together.
  2. Ease it round the curve, pinning as you go.
  3. Sew the inner gusset to the inner edge the same way.
  4. The gussets were cut long — trim the excess as you sew.

4-2. Attach the bottom

  1. Sew the bottom piece to the free edge of each gusset, right sides together.
  2. Check the notches on top and bottom line up before you sew, or the crescent will twist.

4-3. Finish the opening

  1. Turn the allowances at the opening under twice and stitch.
  2. Arrange it so the top and bottom overlap by about 3 cm, making an envelope that stays shut.

4-4. Finish

  1. Turn right side out and press the seams.
  2. Put the pillow in and check the fit.
  3. If the overlap gapes, add two pairs of snaps.

✏️ Cut the gussets long. Easing them round a curve always takes a little more than the calculation says, and cutting them exactly leaves you short in the last few centimeters with nothing to do but start again. Trimming the excess afterwards costs nothing.

5. Tips & variations

  • Make two: a nursing pillow is used daily and gets grubby. Two lets you rotate.
  • Soft fabrics: the baby's face rests on it. Double gauze or terry.
  • Pregnancy too: the same pattern covers a body pillow of the same shape.
