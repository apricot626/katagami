Poncho · step 4-1

Press the shoulder seams open

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3

1Open it out, wrong side up

Open the pieces out and lay them wrong side up on the ironing board. At the seam, the two seam allowances stand up together.

2Fold the allowances apart

Split the two allowances with your fingers and fold one to each side, so the seam sits in the middle. This is called pressing the seam open.

3Press from above

Press the opened allowances flat with the iron. Lift and set the iron down rather than sliding it, so the fabric does not stretch.

Fleece and wool are heat-sensitive: use a low setting and a press cloth.
