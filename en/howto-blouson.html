How to make a blouson jacket

A light jacket pulled in by ribbing at the hem and cuffs. Two fronts, one back, two sleeves and the ribbing. Close it with a separating zip or with snaps if zips are not your favorite. Nylon, twill or denim all have the body this shape needs.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (size M)

  • Main fabric (nylon, twill or denim) — about 110 cm wide × 180 cm (43 × 71 in)
  • Ribbing for the hem and cuffs — about 30 × 90 cm (12 × 35 in)
  • Separating zip — to the finished body length

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Trace the back, fronts, sleeves and the two ribbing pieces.
  2. Cut
    Cut one back, two fronts, two sleeves, two cuff ribbings and one hem ribbing.
  3. Finish the edges
    Serge or zigzag the shoulder, side and underarm allowances.

4. Sewing

4-1. Shoulders, sleeves and sides

  1. Sew both shoulder seams and press the allowances open.
  2. Match the center of each sleeve head to the shoulder seam and sew the sleeves in.
  3. Sew from the cuff along the underarm and down the side in one continuous line.

4-2. Attach the ribbing

  1. Sew each cuff ribbing into a loop, fold it in half lengthwise and sew it to the cuff, stretching it to fit.
  2. Fold the hem ribbing in half lengthwise and sew it to the hem, stretching evenly. It is shorter than the hem — that is what makes the jacket blouse.

4-3. Insert the zip

  1. Sew one side of the separating zip to each front edge. Line the bottom stops up with each other before you start, or one side will finish higher than the other.
  2. Turn the neckline under twice and stitch.

✏️ Cut ribbing to about 85% of the edge it goes on. Stretching that shorter piece evenly as you sew is exactly what pulls the hem in and gives a blouson its shape.

5. Tips & variations

  • If zips put you off: snaps or hook-and-loop close it just as well. Make it that way first and add a zip to the second one.
  • Water-repellent fabric: in coated nylon this becomes a light rain jacket.
  • Add pockets: slash pockets caught in the side seams are worth the extra ten minutes.
