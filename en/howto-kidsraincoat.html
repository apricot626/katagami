How to make a kids' raincoat

A hooded, front-opening raincoat in laminated fabric. The back is cut longer so it goes over a school backpack. Pins leave permanent holes, so use clips throughout.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one coat, 130 cm height)

  • Laminated fabric (lightweight) — about 110 cm wide × 180 cm (43 × 71 in)
  • Snap fasteners — 5 or 6 pairs
  • Seam-sealing tape (optional) — about 3 m
  • A Teflon foot, if you have one
  • Clips instead of pins

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Trace on the wrong side. Chalk marks stay on laminate — choose an unobtrusive color.
  2. Cut the pieces
    Back on the fold, 2 fronts, 2 sleeves, 2 hood pieces. The back is longer than the front.
  3. Transfer the notches
    Mark the armhole and the sleeve head.
  4. Use clips
    Pin holes let water through. Clips only.

4. Sewing

4-1. Shoulders and sleeves

  1. Place front and back right sides together and sew both shoulders.
  2. Match the sleeve head notch to the shoulder seam and set the sleeves in.
  3. Laminate sticks to the presser foot. Without a Teflon foot, sew through a strip of tracing paper and tear it away afterwards.

4-2. Sides and underarms

  1. Place front and back right sides together and sew from cuff to hem in one run.
  2. Unpicking leaves holes, so clip everything first and check the shape before you sew.

4-3. Attach the hood

  1. Sew the two hood pieces together along the back curve.
  2. Hem the face opening.
  3. Sew the lower edge of the hood to the neckline, right sides together.

4-4. Front edges, hem and snaps

  1. Turn the front edges under twice and stitch. A deep overlap keeps the rain out.
  2. Hem the bottom and the cuffs the same way.
  3. Fit 5 or 6 pairs of snaps down the front.
  4. Seal the seams with tape and it will hold up to real rain.

✏️ Pins leave holes in laminate and water comes through them. Use clips. The same goes for unpicking, so clip everything, check the shape, and only then run the seam.

5. Tips & variations

  • A longer back: 8 cm or more if it has to go over a backpack.
  • Reflective tape: visibility is poor in rain. Add it to the cuffs and hem.
  • A bag to match: a drawstring bag in the same fabric lets it be packed away wet.
