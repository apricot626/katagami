How to make a bandana cap

A triangular cap that ties at the back. It hugs the head, which suits cooking, cleaning, and any time the hair needs covering. A short length of elastic across the back makes tying easy.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (one cap, standard size)

  • Fabric (Oxford cloth, linen or double gauze) — about 55 × 45 cm
  • Flat elastic, 1 cm — about 14 cm
  • Sewing thread

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Lay the pattern on the wrong side and trace both lines.
  2. Cut the pieces
    Two body pieces and two ties.
  3. Transfer the notches
    Mark where the curve ends. Below that point you do not sew.

4. Sewing

4-1. Sew the body

  1. Place the two pieces right sides together and sew the curved edge that passes over the head.
  2. Stop at the notch — below it stays open, which is the back opening.
  3. Clip the curve, press the allowance open.

4-2. Hem the face edge

  1. Turn the straight front edge under by 1 cm, then 1.5 cm, and stitch.
  2. This edge shows, so keep the fold even.

4-3. Add the elastic

  1. Turn the lower back edge under twice, threading elastic through as you go.
  2. About 14 cm. Try it on and adjust so it does not slip.
  3. Backstitch over both ends of the elastic.

4-4. Attach the ties

  1. Fold each tie in four and topstitch.
  2. Stitch one to each end of the back edge.
  3. Put it on and knot the ties at the back.

✏️ The elastic across the back means the ties do not have to be pulled tight. Elastic alone squeezes the head; ties alone work loose during the day. A little of each is what makes it wearable for hours.

5. Tips & variations

  • For cooking: it covers the hair properly, which keeps it out of the food.
  • Match an apron: made in the same fabric as the apron or cafe apron, it becomes a set.
  • For medical use: it also serves for covering hair during treatment. Choose something soft against the skin.
