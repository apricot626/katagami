How to make a health-record book case

A bi-fold case that keeps a child health record, appointment cards, an insurance card and scan photos in one place. Body and inner pockets is the whole construction, and stitching the pocket vertically turns it into card slots. It works just as well as a passbook or medicine-record case.

1. Materials & tools

Materials (A6 size)

  • Main fabric (oxford, linen or canvas) — about 60 × 40 cm (24 × 16 in)
  • Lining fabric (light cotton) — the same amount
  • Pocket fabric — about 40 × 25 cm (16 × 10 in)
  • Firm fusible interfacing — for the main fabric
  • Snap fastener or elastic — 1

Tools

  • Sewing machine (or needle and thread)
  • Fabric marker or pencil
  • Ruler and scissors
  • Iron
  • Pins or clips

3. Cutting the fabric

  1. Trace the pattern
    Trace the body and the inner pockets, and mark the center fold on the body.
  2. Cut
    Cut the body in main and lining, and one or two pocket pieces.
  3. Apply interfacing
    Fuse firm interfacing to the main fabric. Loaded up, this case is heavy, and without interfacing it collapses.

4. Sewing

4-1. Make the pockets

  1. Turn the top edge of each pocket under twice and stitch. Always do this first — it cannot be done once the pocket is attached.
  2. If you are using two, make them different heights so the contents are easy to tell apart.

4-2. Attach the pockets

  1. Lay the pockets on the right side of the lining, aligning the lower and side edges.
  2. For card slots, stitch vertically now. About 6 cm (2⅜ in) per card is right. Backstitch at the top of each line — that is where the pocket tears.
  3. Baste the lower and side edges within the seam allowance.

4-3. Sew the layers together

  1. Place main and lining right sides together and sew around, leaving a 10 cm (4 in) gap.
  2. Trim the corners, turn through the gap and press.
  3. Topstitch 5 mm from the edge all the way around.

4-4. Fold and add the closure

  1. Fold at the center line and press.
  2. Put the actual contents in and check how it closes before fitting a snap, or run an elastic band around the spine.

✏️ The thickness setting matters more than it looks. A record book, appointment cards and an insurance card together come to nearly 1.5 cm. Leave that out and the case is pulled open by its own contents.

5. Tips & variations

  • Stagger the pockets: card slots at different heights let you see at a glance what is where.
  • Add a pen loop: caught in the side seam, so there is always a pen at appointments.
  • Also a passbook case: same size, different pocket divisions.
